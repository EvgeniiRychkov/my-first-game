Paint the Grid

Move with the arrow keys and paint every cell green.
Aliens paint cells red. If an alien touches you, you lose a life.
Picking up the weapon (🔫) gives you 3 shots; ammo (📦) gives 3 more. Items vanish after 10 s (the bar shows the time left). Press Space: the bullet flies one cell, toward a neighbouring alien.
Paint every cell green to finish a level. There are 6 levels.
